# Expert portraits

These five portraits depict fictional AI experts. They were generated with OpenAI image generation for xPerfect, then encoded as small 384 × 384 WebP assets. They do not depict employees or imply professional credentials or endorsement.

Art direction: a varied fictional cast with distinct ages, faces, hair and styling. Use cohesive cinematic head-and-shoulders photography, natural skin texture, luminous faces and softly blurred architectural settings with real depth. Restrained teal, blue, amber, coral and violet lighting complements the shared palette. Avoid flat studio gradients, busy backgrounds, text and trademarks. Check the actual 88 px desktop and 80 px narrow-screen crops in both themes. Generation source images and detailed working records remain outside the public source tree.

The expert picker maps these presentation assets only to the five stable public starter IDs. The mapping affects appearance, not worker identity, model, account, skills, authority or catalog content hashes. Custom saved experts use their supplied name and role, with a neutral non-letter fallback when no known portrait exists. Do not infer a profession or portrait from a person's name.
