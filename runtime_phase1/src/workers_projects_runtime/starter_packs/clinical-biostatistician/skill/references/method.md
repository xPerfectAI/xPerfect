## Calculation contract

data.csv needs participant_id, arm and response. Exactly the two plan arms are permitted. Binary response is0/1; blank is missing. The plan must explicitly accept exclude_with_warning. Report assigned/observed/missing counts. Wilson95 intervals are computed per independent group. The Newcombe difference interval combines Wilson limits; this does not solve missing-data bias or establish causality. No p-value/significance claim is generated. A different estimand or model requires a different approved analysis.
