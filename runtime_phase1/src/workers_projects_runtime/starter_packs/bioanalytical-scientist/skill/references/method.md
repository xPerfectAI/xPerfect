## Calculation contract

data.csv has unique sample_id, kind(standard/control/unknown), nominal, signal and dilution. The explicit linear_unweighted_demo method fits an unweighted straight line. This is suitable only for the supplied demonstration method; many real assays require other models or weighting. At least3 varying standards and positive slope/dilutions are required. Control limits come from plan.json; outside-range values are flagged without pretending extrapolation is validated. The full run remains review_required even when all calculations work.

Inputs and this aid are intentionally bounded. Do not apply a demonstrator to a different method merely because it runs. Public third-party sources are optional, pinned references, not automatically connected tools or proof of equivalence.
