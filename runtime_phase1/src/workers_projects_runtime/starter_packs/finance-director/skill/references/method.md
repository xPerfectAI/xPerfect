## Calculation contract

data.csv contains positive actual/budget measures for explicit revenue/expense account types, grouped by month and currency. This is management reporting, not a full audited general ledger. Every month/account pair is unique. Favorable variance uses revenue(actual-budget) and expense(budget-actual). plan.json gives opening cash, separately reported bank/ledger balances and explicit forecast receipts/payments. Cash difference is unresolved until supported, not silently plugged. Forecasts are scenarios; a mixed-sign/mixed-currency or unmapped account needs an approved policy before computation.

Inputs and this aid are intentionally bounded. Do not apply a demonstrator to a different method merely because it runs. Public third-party sources are optional, pinned references, not automatically connected tools or proof of equivalence.
